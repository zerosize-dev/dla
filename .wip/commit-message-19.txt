An alloc-test decides a finding only on a harness that is about it

A real unattended run on a Python library measured the documented leak
line cleanly in the loop over the project's tests: 299 blocks more at each
of nine rounds, gate passed. It added a finding at the line. The gate's fix
said to make a probe driver for the finding and to alloc-test that driver.
The agent skipped the driver and ran `dla alloc-test H5 --finding F572` on
a batch driver made for three other findings. That driver never touches
the line and its series had no column of the finding. The alloc-test fell
back to the whole-process column of the finding's region, which was flat,
answered "non-leak", and `dla ledger refute` accepted that evidence. The
run ended COMPLETE with no leak confirmed, and the leak line was nowhere in
its report.

`dla alloc-test <H> --finding <F>` is now refused with `harness-not-about`,
before anything runs, unless the harness is about the finding: the finding
is among its targets or their siblings, the harness is its entry-path or
probe harness, a trend of a series of the harness has a column tied to the
finding (its finding, a candidate, or the finding's site for an
allocation-site column), or the finding cites growth evidence of a series
of the harness. The fix names the harnesses that are about the finding and
the command for a probe driver of its own.

`dla ledger refute` and `dla ledger confirm` refuse to cite the alloc-test
evidence of a harness that is not about the finding. That covers an
alloc-test run without `--finding`, whose evidence names no finding.

On a harness that is about the finding, the whole-process fallback is not
used for a finding that has an allocation-site column of its own in a trend
of any harness of the run. When that column is missing from the new series,
the coverage of the run decides: the function of the line ran and none of
its blocks stayed, which is a non-leak reading, or the unit does not reach
the line, which is inconclusive and says so.

Replayed on a copy of that run's state: the alloc-test on the unrelated
harness is refused and writes no series, and its old evidence can no longer
be cited to refute the finding.

Seven tests of the non-heap readings alloc-tested a finding on a harness
that was not about it; their fixtures now declare the target or cite the
growth evidence, and what they assert is unchanged.

Not seen yet: an unattended run with this change. The coverage reading is
tested for Python only.
