A capture series has one verdict, and its measured growth stays open until decided

A real run on a Rust library took the allocation stacks of its driver at 1, 2 and 4 times the step.
`dla summarize alloc-stack-diff` passed two stack columns at project sites. `dla trend` of the same
series failed every column, and the run ended "no leak confirmed" after 8 of 167 minutes, with the
growth closed as "inconclusive" by a hypothesis.

The chain that lost the growth applies to the capture summaries of every runtime (allocation stacks,
heap graphs, type histograms):

- `dla trend` judged a series `dla summarize` wrote by the eight-point slope gate. Three captures
  always fail "n < 8", and an exact Mann-Kendall test at three points cannot reach p < 0.05.
- The final gate then offered the failing trend record to `dla ledger add`, so the finding had no
  site.
- The summary series named no harness, so an alloc-test of the driver for that finding was refused
  as `harness-not-about`.
- Nothing made the finding a lead, so a close by text was accepted.

What changes:

- `dla trend` judges a series of source kind `dump` by the capture rule `dla summarize` applied to
  it: one function both commands call, over the series' own x.
  - A column passes in the trend exactly when it passed in the summary. A bytes column and its
    block-count column have one verdict.
  - The count column of one block that is resized reads `resized`, not flat.
  - The report's thresholds say three points, and the evidence names the rule.
  - A rise that stopped still fails and does not read as growing.
  - Harness series keep the eight-point gate unchanged.
- A summary series names the harness when every capture it read lies under that one harness. It is
  about that harness (an alloc-test of it can decide a finding made from the series). It is never a
  run of it: attempts, executed counts, the queue, budget rules and residual items read runs only.
- The final gate lists only capture columns that passed and tie to no finding. Its fix offers the
  passing record and names the alloc-test on the harness.
  - `dla summarize` prints the same `dla ledger add --origin growth-series --evidence <E>` command
    for every summary kind.
  - The finding takes the site of the largest passing column.
  - One finding ties both the bytes and the count column of a capture.
- That finding is a lead when the captures came from a hook driver, and either the finding was made
  from the series or the driver was made for it.
  - `dla ledger resolve --as unresolved` by text is then refused while budget is above its floor,
    and the refusal names the alloc-test.
  - A passed dump column that names no finding leads to the finding now at its site.
  - A later trend of the series does not bring back a lead that a decision lifted.
  - Captures of the project's own workload, or of a driver made for other findings, stay no lead.
- A finding made from a capture accounts for its growing stacks only while it is open, once it is
  decided, or when it was closed for time at the budget floor or for an environment blocker.
- The report's "also growing" lists only columns that passed.
