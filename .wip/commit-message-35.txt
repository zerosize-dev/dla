An alloc-test leaves out a purge that cannot release a column, and lengthens a window that alone keeps it from deciding

Two verdicts of `dla alloc-test` flipped under load and cost CI rounds.

- A purge frees heap: `trim` and `trim-native-heap` the malloc heap, `gc`
  the managed heap, `free-os-memory` and the allocator purges their own
  heaps. None of them releases file-backed pages (the binary, its
  libraries, mapped files) or lowers a peak or a reservation. So for
  `os.rss_file`, `os.vm_hwm`, `os.vm_data`, `native.peak_bytes` and
  `go.total` a purge always reads "retained" and says nothing. Such a
  purge is now left out (`applies: false` in the evidence), and the
  column is judged on its plateau alone: a plateau that cannot decide
  gives an inconclusive column, not a leak. Before, a slow rise of
  file-backed memory on a flat run, read as an undecided plateau, voted
  leak through its "retained" purge. Growth that continues still reads
  leak. A file-backed leak too slow for the plateau test now reads
  inconclusive, never non-leak.
- When a plateau with a purge that kept the growth is held back only by
  a window too short for three expected rises, the alloc-test runs the
  extra points again with the window that would decide (the number of
  points it used to print as a hint), at most twice and to eight times
  the first window, and only when the deadline allows it. Each widening
  is named in the limitations; a refusal for time keeps the first result.

Under three busy loops on four cores, 20 runs each: the JVM test that ran
a command's workload unbound failed 1 of 20 before and 0 of 20 after; the
file-backed test failed 0 of 20 before and after (its failure needs a
chance pass of the first trend, so it is pinned by unit tests built from
the record of the CI failure). The whole dynamic acceptance passes, and
the JVM pool fixture with the same mechanism passed alone 3 of 3.

Not covered. `os.rss_shmem` and `os.swap` have the same mismatch and were
not changed. A widened window on a runtime that is still warming up can
read continuing growth: the plateau rule working as it should, but the
widening is not free.
