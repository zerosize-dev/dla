Growth measured over dumps stays a lead, and the Node heap graph shows who keeps it

A real run on a Node library measured its documented leak, about 15 KB per request, and lost
it in two ways. `dla summarize heap-graph` tied the growth to holders of a per-request object,
and every discriminator favored leak. `dla ledger close-rest` then closed those findings as "not
ranked, not targeted". The model read the snapshots with its own shortest-path script, and every
path went through the agent's own WeakRef list, so it called the growth a measuring artifact. It
stopped at 47 of 180 minutes, facing 154 open items, 60 of them the same 20 listener columns
listed once per run of one harness.

- Growth a dump series measured is a lead (`grown::dump_grown`). It counts for a leak-side root
  that is open, or closed `unresolved` or `suspected_static_only`: a passing growth record of
  authority `dump` with a passed column that names a finding, or a dump discriminator that
  favors leak. `close-rest` leaves it, `ledger resolve` refuses it, and the gate lists it with
  the summarizer's own route (sibling, refute, test, confirm). That holds even when an earlier
  close-rest closed it. It is lifted by a newer dump discriminator that does not favor leak, or
  a newer alloc-test bound to the finding that favors non-leak. An alloc-test that favors leak
  does not lift it: on a driver not made for the finding it gives no confirm route, and the dump
  is about the holder. The harness comes from the capture files of the dumps. Every dump
  summarizer prints this route, so the rule is not Node's.
- The Node agent takes its captures right after its forced collection and before it reads any
  WeakRef in the job (the listener sweep, the listener count, the probes). A `deref()` keeps its
  target on the job's list, and V8 writes that list into the snapshot as a strong-roots edge to
  every tracked emitter. The values are read as before, after one more collection when a
  capture was taken, so the capture's own garbage is not counted.
- `dla summarize heap-graph` (Node) prints `kept_by`, the short retention path of up to three
  passing holders, and `root`, what the root is: a native handle, a module variable, a global, a
  registration. For a native handle that keeps a bound function or closure of the project, it
  gives the line the function is defined at, from the snapshot's locations table, the
  `scripts.json` sidecar and source maps. Only a project file gives a line. `native_hint` carries
  that line, with the `dla ledger annotate --site` command of the tied finding, in a sentence the
  bounded output never cuts.
- The final gate lists a growing per-site column once per harness, on the newest trend, naming
  the older trends in which it grew. A finding whose fix is its confirm with its evidence
  (`dla ledger confirm <F> --evidence <E>`) ranks with the measured growth, not behind the
  open items.

On the ledger of the real run, the gate now has 68 actions instead of 155, and the four closed
findings of the dump growth come back as actions three to six. A replay of the run's driver with
the new agent has no job-keep edge in its snapshots. The same shortest-path script now shows
the holder as a native handle, then a parser, then a bound callback, then the object. The new
summary names the project line of that callback.
