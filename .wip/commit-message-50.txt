An allocation capture sees the state while it is alive; a blind capture is no capture

A real run on a Rust library ended COMPLETE after 4 of 167 minutes with its leak measured and never
attributed. The driver's own count of live bytes rose from 2.68 to 6.49 MB across the captures,
while the allocator shim's exit record read 2.51 MB at every one: exit() frees the calling
thread's thread-locals, the atexit state and the C++ statics before the shim's destructor runs, so
state held there is gone from the record. The flat capture then counted as "the capture", and one
free-text `inconclusive` closed the measured growth.

The shim takes its record while the state is alive:

- The shim exports `dla_shim_snapshot(x)`. It writes the one record of the process now, with
  `"at":"snapshot"` and the driver's x; the destructor and the SIGTERM path then write nothing.
  Every record says when it was taken (`exit`, `snapshot` or `signal`).
- The Rust and C drivers `dla harness gen` writes call it after the last unit and checkpoint,
  before their state is dropped (looked up by name at run time; no shim, nothing happens).
- On the real library of the run, its driver rebuilt with the call: the snapshot read 3.54, 3.54,
  4.52 and 6.49 MB live at four sizes, the same as the driver's own count; the exit record of the
  same driver read 2.51 MB at all four.

A capture is checked against the process's own readings:

- `dla harness run` keeps the measured process's last checkpoint (x, live bytes, live blocks) with
  its shim record.
- `dla summarize` marks a capture series blind when the driver's live bytes rose past a floor
  (64 KiB and 10% of the first reading) and the shim's total rose by less than half of that. It
  prints both series and names the snapshot, or, after a snapshot that is still blind, the
  alloc-test. The shim's peak rising with a flat live total is a hint only.
- A blind summary is no capture for the gates. Of the allocation-stack summaries of a harness the
  newest decides, by the ledger's own order.
- "Capture a point with more work" is gone; the "factor of two" advice is given only when the
  captures differ by less than that.

A measured heap growth is decided by a measurement:

- `ledger resolve O<n> --as unresolved` is refused with `heap-growth-undecided` while the budget is
  over its floor, a heap row of the item is above its threshold, its series passed a live heap
  column, and neither a capture that shows the growing stack nor an alloc-test on the harness
  decided it. The refusal names `dla alloc-test <H> --finding <F>` for each lead, else
  `dla alloc-test <H>` (after the capture when none was summarized).
- A remedy for an item with rows of several series above their thresholds names each series
  (`remedy-misses-series`).
- The gate text of the item says what the capture already showed instead of asking for it again.

A no-probe target of a harness whose heap grew is a lead:

- `dla trend` reads a declared heap target with no probe, whose writer lines ran, in a harness
  whose series passed a live heap column that nothing ties to a declared target, as `grew_untied`,
  not `executed`. `dla trend` puts its alloc-test first; the queue keeps it pending, and close-rest
  and the final gate hold it while the budget is over its floor. It is never credited to the
  finding and never confirms it.

Outputs that drop what matters say so:

- `dla trend` lists the live heap columns first and prints the count of passed columns per
  region. A cut array is named as `<key>_cut` with the number of items left out, for the list
  that is printed. `run status` gives up its budget and revisit list last.

Small fixes:

- Cargo `benches/` directories are not product code in the ranking.
- The second-look slot text says to drop an object early and never forget or leak it, and
  `dla harness run` warns when a Rust driver's own code calls `mem::forget`, `Box::leak` or
  `ManuallyDrop::new`.

A new fixture pair keeps the class: worker threads keep notices in a per-thread outbox when a
fixed-size hand-off is full, and the hand-off empties only when an uplink is attached (the decoy
attaches it).
