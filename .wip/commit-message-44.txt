A step late in the extra window is growth, and a too-short window does not decide

An unattended run on a Rust library built a driver made for the library's documented leak, a
`Vec` field that grows once a bounded ring is full, and measured its growth: the allocator
shim's live bytes rose in steps, 4.5 KB per unit. `dla alloc-test` on that driver then read the
column as a plateau, the finding was refuted, and the run ended COMPLETE after 5 of 167 minutes
with no leak confirmed.

A `Vec` doubles its capacity, so its live bytes rise in steps that come ever further apart. The
alloc-test's extra window held the next step: six points flat at 17.4 MB, then 33.1 MB. The
plateau test fits the extra window with Theil-Sen, and six flat points against two high ones give
a slope of zero. The reading of the steps, which sees the new maximum, ran only when that fitted
rise was over the noise. So it never ran, and the column read as a plateau.

- The plateau test of a gauge that grew in separate steps reads the steps whatever the fitted rise
  of the extra window is. An extra window that ends above the main window's maximum by more than
  the noise, the column's smallest rise and the drift floor is growth that continues. A spike that
  comes back down is not.
- A staircase stopped only once it stays flat for longer than its next gap is expected to be.
  When each gap is at least the one before, as for a capacity that doubles, that is twice the
  last gap plus one point spacing; otherwise the longest gap. A shorter flat stretch is
  inconclusive, and the alloc-test names the extra points that reach past the expected step
  (`--extra`), lengthening its own window when the time fits.
- An inconclusive alloc-test on a driver made for the finding that names a longer extra window
  than it ran no longer decides the growth while the budget is over its floor. The finding stays
  a lead, with `dla alloc-test <H> --finding <F> --extra <n>` first. `dla ledger resolve` refuses
  the close, the final gate lists it and the Stop hook holds the stop. The alloc-test records
  `points_needed` per column and for its result. Older records, a window that ran that long, or
  a budget at its floor keep the old rule.

Not covered: the run also closed 36 candidates of the two lowest tiers as not exercised with 162
minutes left, which the gate allows.
